Boss & Mega-Boss Mechanics
Spawning
One boss guaranteed per dungeon level — the first monster spawned on a level is flagged as boss. _bossSpawned prevents a second boss on the same floor.
Mega-Boss chance: 10% — rolled when the boss spawns. Normal boss otherwise.
Stats at Spawn
Stat	Normal Boss	Mega-Boss
HP	Base HP × 5	Base HP × 10
Defense	Base defense + dungeon level	Flat dungeon level × 2 (replaces base)
Attack bonus (bossAtkBonus)	+dungeon level to all damage rolls	+dungeon level × 2 to all damage rolls
Name prefix	👑 [Title] MonsterName	💀 Mega [Title] MonsterName
The bossAtkBonus is added into a shared _getEnemyDamageMod(enemy) function that also accumulates active-effect bonuses — it feeds every attack that enemy makes.

Base HP/defense before these multipliers come from the monster's ENEMY_TYPES definition, scaled by dungeon level (the same formula as regular monsters of that type).

Boss Titles are picked from a tag-matched pool:

Monster Tag	Title Pool
undead	Ancient, Dread, Wailing, Cursed, Spectral
demon	Infernal, Abyssal, Hellborn, Dread
humanoid	Warchief, Overlord, Champion, Warlord
beast	Dire, Elder, Ancient, Great
construct	Masterwork, Ancient, Runed
aberration	Elder, Void-touched, Abominable
monster	Greater, Elder, Ancient
(default)	Dread, Elder, Greater, Ancient
Actions Per Round
Enemy Type	Actions/Round
Normal enemy	1
Boss	2
Mega-Boss	3
Each action is a full independent attack turn. This makes bosses dramatically more dangerous even before considering their inflated stats.

Stun Mechanics — Two Separate Systems
There are two independent stun checks in the code:

1. Player-inflicted stuns (_tryStunEnemy) — called by melee, magic, weapon riders (lightning/ice), Quivering Palm, Faerie Queen hold, Bard Dissonant Chord, all summon abilities, etc.

Boss: 100% immune (always blocked, no random roll)
Mega-Boss: 100% immune (always blocked)
Message: "[Name] is immune to stun!"
2. In-turn stun resist (_executeOneEnemyTurn) — applied when the enemy's turn arrives and it happens to be stunned (can happen via very specific non-_tryStunEnemy paths):

Boss: 50% chance to shrug it off ("resists the stun with sheer force!")
Mega-Boss: 80% chance to shrug it off ("resists the stun with terrifying might!")
In practice the first system means bosses should never become stunned from player actions, but the second system acts as a secondary safety net.

Immunity to Player Abilities
Ability	Effect on Bosses/Mega-Bosses
Rogue Backstab instakill	Fully immune — deals normal (non-instakill) damage instead
Paladin Smite purge (instakill)	Immune to instant death — instead takes ×4 smite damage
Paladin AoE Smite purge	Same ×4 damage redirect (at 1/3 the base smite damage)
Bard Charm Monster	Fully immune — cannot be charmed at all
Any player-inflicted stun	Fully immune (see above)
All stun-adjacent effects (Faerie Queen hold, lightning/ice riders, QP)	Immune via _tryStunEnemy gate
Loot on Death
Category	Normal Enemy	Boss	Mega-Boss
Gold multiplier	×1	×5	×10
Loot roll passes	1	5	5
Trinket rolls	1 at base chance	1 at 3× chance	5 rolls at base chance
Guaranteed rare reagents	0	1–2	1–2
Guaranteed Epic reagents (DL 15+)	0	1	2
Guaranteed Legendary (DL 20+)	0	1	2
Guaranteed Mythic (DL 25+)	0	1	2
Guaranteed Divine (DL 30+)	0	1	2
"5 loot roll passes" means every loot table (food, potions, weapons, armor, shields, reagents, torches) is independently rolled 5 times — so a boss can drop 5 weapons or 5 potions.

XP on Death
Enemy Type	XP formula
Normal	XP_PER_MONSTER_LEVEL (10) × enemy level × 1
Boss	10 × enemy level × 1 (no XP multiplier — same as normal)
Mega-Boss	10 × enemy level × **4**
Bosses give normal XP. Only Mega-Bosses grant a 4× XP bonus.

Damage Formula Summary
A boss on dungeon level 10 attacking:

Rolls its base monster damage (from ENEMY_TYPES, scaled by its enemy level)
Adds bossAtkBonus (+10 for a boss, +20 for a mega-boss at DL10)
Adds any active-effect damage modifiers (e.g. bard inspire)
That total hits a party member, reduced by their defense, then armor blocks apply
Because mega-boss defense is DL×2 flat (replacing base defense rather than adding to it), a mega-boss at DL 1 actually has less defense than a normal boss (2 vs. base + 1) — but from DL 5+ the flat formula overtakes typical base defenses and quickly becomes extreme at deeper levels.
