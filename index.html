Light & Darkness
The dungeon is genuinely dark — only what your party carries is lit. You start with 3 torches. When your light goes out, return to the Tinkerer or restock from monster drops.
🔦 Torch — 12-square radius, 10 minutes, warm flickering flame. Buy for 25g; 3% chance to drop from any slain monster.
🫙 Lantern + 🛢️ Lantern Oil — 24-square radius, 15 minutes per oil, steady golden light. Lantern 100g (reusable), Oil 30g (consumed).
✨ Light spell — mage only. 10 MP for an 14-square cool white light lasting 5 minutes.
Press T any time to pick a source. Only one light burns at a time; starting a new one replaces the old. Timers only tick during exploration (combat pauses them).
Stat Bars
Red — Health (HP). When this reaches 0 the character falls.
Green — Stamina (ST). Powers melee and ranged attacks.
Blue — Mana (MP). Powers magic attacks.
Purple — Experience (XP). Fills as your character earns XP; resets each time they level up.
Attacking with insufficient stamina/mana still works, but deals half damage.
Levels & XP
Every character starts at Level 1 and can reach Level 100.
Defeating a monster grants 10 × its level XP, split evenly across all living party members (summons excluded).
Cumulative XP needed for level N is 100 × N² (400 for L2, 900 for L3, 1,600 for L4, …).
On level-up: +12 HP / +12 ST / +12 MP (scaled by class modifiers), and all pools refill to full.
Each class gains per-level scaling (see Classes below) — higher levels grant better damage, stun/crit/dodge chances, and special bonuses.
Combat Rows
Every character is either Front Row (⚔) or Back Row (🏹). Toggle from the personal inventory screen.
Monsters only target front-row characters with melee attacks. However, ranged and spell attacks can reach back-row characters directly.
When the entire front row is down, combat pauses and you must promote a back-row ally forward.
Only Rogues may melee from the back row (stealth). Everyone else is limited to ranged / magic / special abilities from the back.
Initiative & Turn Order
At the start of each combat, every participant — allies and enemies alike — rolls a d12 to determine turn order. Higher rolls act first; ties are broken randomly.
Class bonuses: Rogues, Monks, and Rangers roll at +1 initiative.
The initiative order is fixed for the whole combat — the same sequence repeats every round (dead combatants are simply skipped).
Summons created mid-combat inherit their summoner's initiative and are inserted immediately after the caster's slot — they act this round, not the next.
Combat Mechanics
Defense = class + species + per-level bonus + armor + trinkets. Every point directly reduces incoming damage.
Melee attacks have a 20% base stun chance; warriors gain +3% per level.
Ranged attacks have a 20% base crit chance; rangers gain +3% per level. Ranged weapons roll +1 min/+1 max damage over other subtypes.
Magic attacks hit random targets — the number of hits equals the caster's level + 4 (mages get +2 extra hits). Mages also gain +1% stun per level.
Rogue Backstab: 3× stamina for 2× damage plus +10% per rogue level (+100% at L10). Rogues can also attempt to spot and disarm traps.
Equipped weapons add their power to matching attack types.
Equipped armor reduces incoming damage by its blocking value. See Armor Restrictions below.
Equipped shields have a 25% chance to completely block an incoming attack. Shields cannot be used with a ranged weapon equipped, and Mages/Monks/Necromancers/Artificers cannot use shields at all.
Warrior Defend Mode (L20) — A warrior in Defend Mode intercepts attacks targeting any party member (melee, ranged, or magic/AoE). The warrior rolls their augmented block chance (shield base + 1%/3 warrior levels); on a hit, the original target takes no damage and the warrior absorbs 10% of the post-defense damage instead. Toggling Defend Mode on/off costs the warrior's action that turn. While in Defend Mode, the warrior's own melee, ranged, and magic attack buttons are all disabled — only special/passive abilities remain available.
Warrior Stun Resistance — Warriors have a passive level × 2% chance to shrug off any stun (caps at 95% at level 47+). A shrugged stun is fully negated and the warrior acts normally that turn.
Encounter size: combat always pulls in enough adjacent monsters to match your party size (summons included).
Fallen allies are not auto-revived at the end of combat — use a Resurrection Potion to bring them back.
Status Effects
🟢 Poison — ticks damage at the start of each of your rounds for several turns. Spiders and some monsters apply it on hit. A bigger dose refreshes and supersedes a weaker one. Poison from traps persists out of combat and continues ticking every 10 seconds of exploration time. Poisoned characters show a green 🤢 face badge on their HUD portrait.
✨ Stun — the target loses its next action. Some character abilities and some monster abilities can stun. Weapon riders (⚡ Lightning and ❄️ Ice) also stun the target for 1 round when they proc. Regular monsters have an increasing chance to shrug off stun. Bosses and Mega-Bosses are immune to stun.
⚡ Shocked (Lightning rider) — stunned for 1 round and attack power reduced for several rounds. Enemy deals less damage while shocked.
❄️ Chilled (Ice rider) — stunned for 1 round and defense reduced for several rounds. Enemy takes extra damage while chilled.
🍃 Entangle (druid) — roots an enemy: its defense is reduced, so your party deals extra damage for the duration.
🧚 Fae Poison (Faerie Queen) — a magic DoT applied by the Faerie Queen's Wrath of Nature. Bypasses physical resistances. Shown as 🧚 badge on enemy card.
🧚 Fae Hold (Faerie Queen) — the Faerie Queen's hold effect stuns an enemy for 2 rounds. Shown as 🧚 Held badge on enemy card.
🎶 Dissonant Chord (Bard, in-combat) — one-use-per-fight AoE: deals magic damage to all enemies, applies -attack/-defense for 1 round, and gives each a 50% stun chance. Scale rises by +1 every 5 bard levels.
Armor & Shield Restrictions
Cloth only: Mage, Necromancer, Monk
Cloth / Leather: Rogue, Druid, Artificer (no chain or plate)
Cloth / Leather / Chain: Ranger, Bard (no plate)
All armor: Warrior, Cleric, Paladin
No shields: Mage, Monk, Necromancer, Artificer. A shield is also forced off whenever you equip a ranged weapon or enable dual-wielding.
Dungeon Levels & Portals
The dungeon has up to 100 floors. Deeper floors spawn tougher monsters that deal more damage and drop more gold.
🔴 Red portal — descends to the next floor. Appears on every level.
🟢 Green portal — ascends to the previous floor. Appears on levels 2 and deeper.
Step through a portal to travel to the next (or previous) dungeon floor.
Exploration
Walk into enemies — or let them walk into you — to begin combat. Monsters in adjacent cells join the fight, scaled to party size.
The Wandering Tinkerer roams the dungeon. Bumping into them opens a shop (no combat).
Rest at any time in safety to recover. Keep food stocked!
⚠️ Traps: invisible pressure plates lurk on some floor tiles. Types are randomised per trap — pit, swinging blade, dart volley, arrow trap, falling rocks, poison spray, acid jet, fire blast, lightning rune, choking gas, ceiling crusher, scything pendulum, snare net, spring spikes. If you have a Rogue in the party, they may spot the trap before you step on it and you can attempt to disarm it — success scales with rogue level. Without a rogue, you trigger the trap and the whole party takes damage. A modal dialog announces every sprung trap, every successful disarm (revealing any hidden treasure), and every failed disarm. Poison-kind traps (darts, poison spray, choking gas) also apply a poison DoT.
Regeneration
All characters slowly regenerate while exploring: +1 HP/min, +3 ST/min, +2 MP/min by default.
Class and species add to these rates. Regen also continues while menus are open, but not during combat.
Food & Hunger
Every non-summoned party member needs to eat (golems and other summons are excluded). Food is checked every 15 minutes of real exploration time.
When the check fires, each hungry character automatically consumes 1 food — drawing first from their personal inventory, then from the group inventory if their personal supply is empty.
If no food is left, hunger escalates through three stages:
🟡 Hungry — minor stat penalty (−1 to all attacks and defense).
🟠 Starving — significant penalty and stamina drain.
🔴 Dying — characters lose HP each minute until they eat or fall.
The hunger badge appears on each character's portrait in the HUD. Buy food at the Tinkerer (50g) or find it as monster loot. Resting also requires food.
Classes
⚔️ Warrior — Front-line tank. +50% HP/ST, no mana. +1 melee, base +1 defense. All armor, shields OK. Per level: +1 melee, +1 defense, +3% stun. Extra attack every 5 levels (L5→+1, L10→+2, L15→+3, L20→+4). Stun Resistance (passive, all levels): level × 2% chance to shrug off stun and act normally (caps at 95%). L20 — Defend Mode (toggle, uses action): adds +1%/3 warrior levels to shield block chance; disables attacks while active. Any attack targeting a party member first checks whether a defending warrior intercepts at their augmented block chance — on success the warrior reduces the raw incoming damage by their own armor and defense, then takes 10% of that remainder (minimum 1). Covers melee, ranged, and magic/AoE. Cannot intercept while stunned, held, petrified, or dead. Multiple defending warriors are each checked independently.
🏹 Ranger — Bowman. +25% HP/ST, -50% mana. +1 ranged. No plate. Shield OK (but not with a bow equipped). Can Summon Woodland Beast (7 MP): wolf, bear, eagle, or pixie. Favored Enemy: choose a monster category — Rangers ignore enemy defense and gain a 1%/3 levels instakill chance vs. their favored type (bosses and mega-bosses are immune to instant death — a triggered instakill deals ×4 ranged damage instead). Track (level 3+): reveals all enemies on the Minimap. Per level: +1 ranged, +3% crit. L20 — Explosive Arrow (6 ST): fires a single detonating arrow that hits all hostile enemies at once for ~67% post-defense ranged damage per target, half crit chance, and half instakill chance vs favored enemies. L20+ — Extra Favored Enemies: unlock one extra favored enemy slot per 5 levels above L15 (+1 at L20, +2 at L25, +3 at L30…). Each slot applies the same ignore-defense and instakill bonus independently.
🪄 Mage — Arcane caster. -25% HP/ST, +50% mana. +1 magic, +2 MP regen. Cloth only, no shield. Magic attacks hit level+6 random targets (with replacement — one enemy may be hit multiple times). Arcane Shield (L3, 8 MP): grants all party members a defense bonus for several rounds; also protects against AoE attacks that hit all rows. Per level: +1 magic, +1% magic stun. L20 — Mirror Image (35 MP): creates floor(level/7) illusory duplicates (2 at L20). Each image absorbs one incoming hit of any type (melee, ranged, magic, or AoE) before shattering — while images remain, all attacks strike a random image instead of the mage. Cannot cast again while images are active. L20 — AoE Crit (passive): each target hit by a magic AoE has a 1% chance per 2 mage levels to be critically struck for ×(2.0+level×0.01) damage (10% at L20).
🗡️ Rogue — Stealthy striker. +100% ST, no mana. +1 melee, +2 ST regen. Cloth or leather armor. Can melee from back row. Backstab: 3× stamina for 2× damage + 10% per rogue level + 5% instakill (+1%/level; bosses and mega-bosses are immune to instant death — a triggered instakill deals ×4 melee damage instead). Can spot and disarm traps. Per level: +1 melee, +1% instakill. L20 — Backstab Bleed: every backstab passively applies a bleed DoT equal to 50% of damage dealt per round, lasting floor(level/5) rounds (4 rounds at L20). The bleed DoT is refreshed/upgraded on repeated backstabs. Immune: undead, constructs, elementals, incorporeal creatures.
🧘 Monk — Martial artist. Neutral stats (no HP/ST/MP modifier). +1 melee, +1 ST/MP regen. Cloth only, no shield. Melee costs both ST & MP. 50% whirlwind. 40% base dodge (2 ST + 2 MP, melee only) — caps at 95%. All incoming damage is also reduced by the monk's current dodge %, so a L1 monk on a non-dodged hit still takes 60% damage. Dodge and damage reduction both require ST > 0 and MP > 0. Per level: +1 melee, +1% whirlwind, +1% dodge. L20 — Quivering Palm: front-row strike for 2× melee damage, then plants an internal DoT on the target. The DoT starts at the base melee roll and doubles every round (bypasses all armor & defense) for 2+ rounds, capped at 10 doublings total (after 10 doublings the damage stays at its peak). Stacking adds +1 round (cap = floor(highestMonkLevel/5), max 10).
✨ Cleric — Healer. +25% HP, -50% ST, +25% mana. All armor, shields OK. Heal: 5 MP → 25% max HP. Mass Heal (L4): 5 MP, heals the whole party at half rate. Revive (L3): 25 MP brings a fallen ally back at 25% HP. Turn Undead (L6, 20 MP): deals 2× magic attack damage to all undead enemies and debuffs their attack & defense for several rounds. Per level: +2% heal power. L20 — Mass Regen (15 MP): applies a healing over time to all living party members (excluding undead/golems). Heals ~10%+/round for floor(level/4) rounds; refreshes on re-cast. L20 — Mass Revive (30 MP): revives up to floor(level/7) fallen allies at 33%+ HP in party order.
💀 Necromancer — Dark caster. -25% HP, -50% ST, +75% mana. Cloth only, no shield. Summon Undead (7 MP, combat only): Skeleton at L1, then stronger tiers unlock every 2 levels: zombie → ghoul → spectre → mummy → ghost → vampire → death knight. All undead gain +1 melee & +1 defense per necromancer level on top of tier scaling. 40% chance to summon a 2nd undead free (horde cascade); each additional roll drops 5% (2nd 40%, 3rd 35%, 4th 30% …), and each necromancer level adds +1% to every roll. Undead act immediately on summon. Upkeep: each living undead minion costs 1 MP per round (drained at the start of each round). All undead immune to poison & stun. Ghoul: 40% paralyze. Spectre & Ghost: incorporeal (bypass armor, immune to web/paralysis/constrict/stun). Ghost: fear up to [necroLevel] enemies (−3 atk/−3 def, permanent, 50% resist). Mummy: Mummy Rot DoT (permanent) + 33% stun. Vampire: life drain + gaseous form on death (immune, regens, revives at 30% HP). Death Knight: 25% melee block, (33+necroLevel)% stun, 5% HP regen/turn, instakill chance. Magic drains HP to caster and own undead. Dark Harvest (combat, uses turn): sacrifice 10% max HP + 10% max Stamina to recover 10% max Mana. Cannot reduce HP to 0. Per level: +1 magic, +1 drain. L20 — Lich Form (toggle, 15 MP/round upkeep): the necromancer transforms into an undying lich. While active: immune to poison and stun; gains 50%+ magic/AoE resistance (scales with level); Summon Undead calls forth one extra undead of the same tier for free. When mana drops to 0 with Lich Form on, stamina auto-converts to mana. On fatal damage, the soul is sealed into a Phylactery — the necromancer cannot act for 3 rounds, then revives at 50%+ HP still in Lich Form (so long as allies remain). If the entire party falls while the necromancer is phylactery-bound, the party is defeated — but the necromancer revives post-combat in mortal form at 10% HP with Lich Form cancelled. Clerics and paladins cannot revive a Phylactery-bound necromancer. Toggle off to stop the mana drain.
🎶 Bard — Inspiring performer. -20% ST, +20% MP. +1 MP regen. Cloth/leather/chain (no plate); shields OK. Out-of-combat (V): activate the Combined Song — party-wide Haste, Battle, and Healing buffs; drains 5 MP/min, cancel any time. In-combat — Dissonant Chord (7 MP, once per fight): hits all enemies with magic damage, debuffs -atk/-def for 1 round, 50% stun chance each. Scale = +1 per 5 levels. Per level: +1 magic damage. L20 — Charm Monster (50 MP): attempt to charm a single enemy to fight for the party. Success chance = 50% + 1% per 2 bard levels (60% at L20, max 95%). Duration = floor(level/5) rounds (4 at L20). Immune: undead, elementals, constructs — and bosses/mega-bosses always resist. Charmed monsters move to your side, fight hostile enemies, and can be healed by clerics (except undead, constructs, and elementals). When the charm expires the monster returns to the enemy ranks.
🌿 Druid — Nature caster. -15% HP/ST, +30% MP. +1 MP regen. Cloth/leather only (no chain or plate); shields OK. Entangle (8 MP): rolls 50% on each enemy to debuff -2 defense & -2 damage (rises by +1 every 2 levels beyond 1); does not affect incorporeal enemies. Summon Woodland Beast (7 MP): wolf, bear, eagle, or pixie — same as ranger. Summon Treant (7 MP, level 5+): a massive ancient tree spirit joins the front row; slams enemies for heavy melee damage and has a 33% chance to hold the target (undead & incorporeal foes are immune to the hold). Per level: +1 magic damage. L20 — Commune (free action): commune with the Fae to gain 1 fae token (shown as ✨ badge on portrait). At 3 tokens, the Faerie Queen is summoned — she joins the back row, has 2× druid max HP, 30+level defense, and attacks with Wrath of Nature: hits floor(druidLevel/4) random targets each turn with magic damage, applying a fae poison DoT and a 33%+ chance to Hold each target for 2 rounds. She has 50% magic and AoE resistance and can be healed by clerics. Tokens persist between rounds; summoning costs no MP but consumes all tokens.
🛡️ Paladin — Holy warrior. +25% HP, neutral ST, -25% MP. All armor, shields OK. Smite (5 MP, front row only): armor-ignoring holy melee strike. Against undead or demon foes the attack gains +2 damage per paladin level, scaled by (2 + 0.1 × level), with a (1% + 1%/level) chance to purge the target — capped at 50%. Against normal enemies Smite does plain melee damage. Boss/Mega-Boss purge resistance: when the purge roll succeeds against a boss or mega-boss, instead of instant death they take ×4 smite damage. Lay On Hands (5 MP): heals the most-injured living ally for 12.5% of their max HP (base), +1% per paladin level. Fire Aura (toggle, free action to activate): reflects all melee hits back at the attacker as fire damage; costs 4 MP/round upkeep; deactivating costs your turn. Per level: +1 melee, +1 defense. Extra attack every 7 levels (L7→+1, L14→+2, L21→+3). L20 — Revive (25 MP): brings a single fallen ally back at 25% HP (mirrors Cleric Revive). L20 — AoE Smite (15 MP, front row only): calls down a holy nova hitting all undead and demon enemies at ⅓ damage with ⅓ purge chance (same 50% cap; bosses take ×4 AoE smite damage instead of instant death).
🪓 Barbarian — Berserker. +60% HP, +40% ST, no mana. +2 melee, +1 HP/+2 ST regen. Cloth or leather armor only; no shields. Rage (once per combat, free action): halves all incoming damage, grants stun immunity, regens 5% max HP per round, adds +1 melee damage per barbarian level, and unlocks +1 bonus melee strike per 3 levels (each costs 3 ST). Rage lasts until end of combat. Barbarians have no mana and cannot use magic or items requiring MP. Per level: +1 melee. L20 — Blood Rage (passive, activates while raging): each melee hit (including rage bonus strikes) generates 1% of max HP as temp HP. Temp HP (shown as 💪 badge) absorbs incoming damage before real HP is deducted. Running out of stamina during extra strikes clears all temp HP and fades the Battle Fury. In addition, while raging with no temp HP buffer, the barbarian enters Wound Mode: at 75% HP → ×2 melee damage, at 50% → ×4, at 25% → ×6. Temp HP resets at the end of combat.
⚙️ Artificer — Tinker-gunner. Neutral stats. Cloth/leather only (no chain or plate); no shields. Scatter Shot replaces the default ranged attack: primary shot hits at full damage, plus 2 splash shots at 50% + 1%/level damage (max 100%) to other enemies (+1 splash every 5 artificer levels). Can forge persistent Golems (unlocked by Artificer level — 🧟 Flesh at L1, 🗿 Clay at L5, 🪨 Stone at L10, 🧾 Iron at L15, ⚙️ Mithril at L20, 🗿 Adamantine at L25, 💫 Divine Soul at L30). Higher-tier golems gain powerful attacks (kinetic waves, armor-piercing bolts, divine strikes), elemental damage resistance, and immunity to stun, poison, and web. All golem stats scale with Artificer Level (AL): melee damage 2–12 + floor(AL×1.5), defense AL×2, and HP (base + per-AL scaling). Repair Golem (1 matching reagent → 50% max HP). Golems persist between combats but die permanently on KO. Advanced Crafting (K): Enchant weapons/armor up to +7 with elemental riders; brew healing potions and powerful scrolls (Warding, Wrath); and forge/repair golems. Higher-tier golem reagent costs are significant — plan your dungeon runs accordingly. Per level: +1 ranged.
Monster Tags & Special Powers
Some monsters carry tags that unlock class interactions. Mouse over an enemy card in combat to see its tag line.
🧟 Undead — vulnerable to paladin Smite (bonus damage & instakill) and cleric Turn Undead (L6, 2× magic damage + debuff to all undead). Necromancer life-drain rules also apply.
😈 Demon — vulnerable to paladin Smite bonus damage and instakill chance.
🐺 Beast — wild creatures; ranger and druid summons count as beasts.
👻 Incorporeal — spectral creatures (fire, air, and water elementals; spectres; ghosts) pass through physical bindings. Cannot be Entangled (druid vines pass through them), cannot be held by treant or ghoul paralysis, and immune to web. They can still be damaged normally. Undead with incorporeal status (spectre, ghost) are also immune to stun and poison. Wolf summon Bleed does not apply to undead enemies.
Boss Monsters
A 👑 Boss may spawn per dungeon level — a crowned variant of a normal monster with dramatically increased power.
Bosses have 5× HP, +dungeonLevel attack and defense. Their name appears with a crown (👑) in the combat log and enemy card. Bosses take 2 actions per round.
Defeating a boss grants 5× gold, 5 loot rolls (weapon, armor, trinket, reagents — all rolled 5 times), and a 3× trinket drop chance per roll. Bosses also guarantee an extra 1–2 rare reagents.
💀 Mega Boss — extremely rare encounter (deeper floors). Mega Bosses are dramatically stronger than regular bosses: massive HP, very high attack and defense, and take 3 actions per round. Defeating a Mega Boss grants 10× gold, 5 guaranteed loot rolls, and 2 of every qualifying reagent tier.
Boss & Mega Boss immunity: Bosses and Mega Bosses are completely immune to stun, hold, and all player-inflicted stun effects (including weapon riders, Monk Quivering Palm hold, Faerie Queen hold, Bard Dissonant Chord, etc.). They are also immune to instant-kill effects — Backstab and Smite/AoE Smite purge still deal heavy bonus damage instead. Plan accordingly: focus on raw damage and party sustain.
Crafting (K)
Press K while exploring to open the Crafting menu (requires an Artificer in the party). Three tabs: Enchant, Potions & Scrolls, Golems.
Reagents come in seven tiers — ✨ Common / 🔵 Uncommon / 🟣 Rare / 🔶 Epic / 🌟 Legendary / 💜 Mythic / 💫 Divine — each rolling independently per monster slain. Common drops everywhere; Uncommon at DL 4+; Rare at DL 7+. Base 5% chance per tier, +1%/level up to 10%. Epic (DL 15+), Legendary (DL 20+), Mythic (DL 25+), and Divine (DL 30+) have a flat 1% drop rate with no level bonus — but bosses guarantee 1 of each qualifying tier, and mega-bosses guarantee 2. Also sold at the Tinkerer (Common / Uncommon / Rare only). Higher tiers unlock stronger golem tiers and enchant levels +4 through +7.
Weapon Enchants — upgrade any equipped weapon to +1 / +2 / +3 (each step flat +damage). After reaching +1 you may attune a single elemental rider (25% proc on melee/ranged hits):
  • 🔥 Fire — 1.5× extra burn DoT for 1 additional round.
  • 🧪 Acid — DoT plus enemy defense debuff.
  • 🟢 Poison — DoT plus enemy damage debuff.
  • ⚡ Lightning — 1-round stun + attack (damage) debuff.
  • ❄️ Ice — 1-round stun + defense debuff.
Armor Enchants — +1 / +2 / +3 to an equipped armor piece (flat bonus defense). Unequipping clears the enchant.
Potions & Scrolls:
  • 🧪 Minor Healing Potion — restores a portion of max HP to one character.
  • 🧪 Greater Healing Potion — restores more HP to one character.
  • 🛡️ Scroll of Warding — read aloud to grant the entire party bonus defense. Bonus = +2 base, +1 per 5 artificer levels (AL5→+3, AL10→+4, AL15→+5 …). Craft cost: 2 common reagents base, +1 common per bonus point above +2; +1 uncommon per +5 total bonus; +1 rare per +10 total bonus. Duration: 5 min + 1 min per 2 artificer levels. An active buff icon appears near the compass with time remaining.
  • 🔥 Scroll of Wrath — read aloud to grant the entire party bonus damage to all attack types. Same bonus scaling and cost formula as Scroll of Warding. Duration: 5 min + 1 min per 2 artificer levels. An active buff icon appears near the compass with time remaining.
Golems (unlocked by Artificer level; all stats scale with AL at time of forging — melee 2–12 + floor(AL×1.5), defense AL×2):
  • 🧟 Flesh Golem (L1, 5 common) — regenerates 10% max HP each round. Immune to poison and stun.
  • 🗿 Clay Golem (L5, 10 common + 3 uncommon) — 25% chance to reflect 50% of incoming damage. Immune to poison and stun.
  • 🪨 Stone Golem (L10, 15 common + 5 uncommon + 1 rare) — every 3rd round, ground-slam hits all enemies (25% stun). Immune to poison and stun.
  • 🤖 Iron Golem (L15, 20 common + 8 uncommon + 3 rare) — cleaves one extra enemy each melee. Immune to poison, stun, and web.
  • ⚙️ Mithril Golem (L20, 30 common + 15 uncommon + 8 rare) — each turn unleashes a kinetic force wave hitting floor(AL/3) enemies at once. +8 HP/AL. Immune to poison, stun, and web.
  • 🗿 Adamantine Golem (L25, 40 common + 25 uncommon + 15 rare + 5 epic + 1 legendary) — fires floor(AL/5) armor-piercing bolts per turn (each crits at 20%+AL/2%); takes half damage from magic, AoE, and ranged. +10 HP/AL. Immune to poison, stun, and web.
  • 💫 Divine Soul Golem (L30, 50 common + 35 uncommon + 25 rare + 10 epic + 5 legendary + 3 mythic + 1 divine) — hits floor(AL/3) enemies with divine melee per turn (ignores half defense, 20% stun each); 33% chance each round to heal every living ally for 10% of the golem's lost HP; takes half damage from magic, AoE, and ranged. +15 HP/AL. Immune to poison, stun, and web.
Only one golem per artificer at a time. Golems are persistent (they travel with you between combats and floors) but die permanently on KO — no revive, no resurrect. The artificer may Repair Golem in or out of combat (spends 1 reagent of the golem's tier → 50% max HP).
Monster Drops
Every slain monster rolls independently for each loot kind — gold, food, healing potion, weapon/armor/shield/trinket drops — as well as:
✨ Magical Reagents — Seven tiers drop from slain monsters; each rolls independently. Common (DL 1+), Uncommon (DL 4+), and Rare (DL 7+) have a base 5% drop chance that grows +1% per dungeon level (capped at 10%). Epic (DL 15+), Legendary (DL 20+), Mythic (DL 25+), and Divine (DL 30+) each have a flat 1% drop rate with no scaling — however bosses guarantee 1 of every qualifying tier, and mega-bosses guarantee 2. Reagents power the Crafting menu (K). Common, Uncommon, and Rare can also be purchased from the Wandering Tinkerer (500g / 2,000g / 5,000g respectively).
Trinkets & Equipment
Each non-summoned character has five extra equipment slots: Cloak, Neck, Ring I, Ring II, and Belt.
Trinkets come in four bonus types — defense / melee / ranged / magic — across four tiers (Common, Uncommon, Rare, Legendary) for +1 to +4.
Dual-aspect trinkets start dropping at Dungeon Level 10. These carry two bonuses: one is always Defense, and the other is Melee, Ranged, or Magic — both at the same tier value. The chance starts at 25% and rises by 1% per dungeon level beyond 10 (reaching 100% at DL 85). Dual-aspect trinkets are loot-only (not sold at the shop).
Trinkets are rare loot drops from defeated monsters (2% base chance, scaling with dungeon depth). The Tinkerer stocks a small selection of tier-1 single-aspect trinkets.
Rings can be worn in either ring slot; cloaks/necks/belts each have a dedicated slot.
Summons
All summons act immediately on the round they are called, inheriting the caster's initiative position.
🐺 Wolf — Front-row melee. Bite inflicts Bleed (100% of hit damage per round for 3 rounds). Scales: +7 HP/level, +1 defense/level.
🐻 Bear — High-HP front-row tank. Stun chance = ranger's crit chance. Scales: +2 defense/level, +10 HP/level.
🦅 Eagle — Ranged striker. Crit chance = ranger's crit chance; crits deal 4× damage. Scales: +5 HP/level.
🧚 Pixie — AoE magic, dodge chance = ranger's crit. Fey Nature: takes half damage from AoE magic. Scales: +2 HP/level.
(Ranger and Druid, 7 MP each. Ranger stun/crit/dodge chances scale with ranged crit.)
🌳 Treant (Druid only, level 5+, 7 MP): ancient tree spirit joins the front row. Deals heavy slam damage each turn. 33% chance to hold the struck target for 1 round (skips their next turn). Undead and incorporeal foes are immune to the hold.
🧚️ Faerie Queen (Druid L20, 3 Commune tokens): joins the back row. 2× druid max HP, 30+level defense, 50% magic/AoE resistance. Each turn casts Wrath of Nature — hits floor(druidLevel/4) enemies with magic damage, applying fae poison DoT and a 33%+ hold chance per hit. Can be healed by clerics.
🦴 Necromancer undead — see Necromancer class description above for full tier details. All undead act immediately on summon. Ranger & Druid beasts can be healed by clerics. Necromancer undead cannot — but the necromancer's drain restores them.
Artificer Golems are a separate summon family — persistent, one per artificer, permadeath on KO. Only the forging artificer may repair them (1 tier-matched reagent for 50% HP) and they survive between combats. All golem stats scale with the Artificer's level.
Summons appear in the party roster during combat (and can be targeted & healed), and are removed from the roster when combat ends — except for persistent golems, which remain in the roster until they fall.
Species
👤 Human — Well-rounded. +1 HP regen, +1 ST regen.
🧝 Elf — Graceful archer-mages. +1 ranged, +1 magic.
⛏️ Dwarf — Stout mountain-folk. +1 melee, +1 ST regen.
👹 Orc — Brutish warriors. +1 melee, +1 HP regen.
🧙 Gnome — Tinkering illusionists. +1 magic, +1 MP regen.
🧑 Halfling — Nimble wanderers. +1 defense, +1 ST regen.
Items & Shop
🍎 Food (50g) — consumed when resting (1 food per rest). Also each non-summoned party member eats 1 food automatically every 15 minutes of exploration time (drawn first from their personal bag, then from the group inventory). When no food is available a character becomes Hungry → Starving → Dying. Buy food at the Tinkerer or find it as monster loot.
🧪 Healing Potion (50g) — restores full HP to one character.
🕯️ Resurrection Potion (200g) — revives a fallen party member to full HP/ST/MP.
Artificer-crafted potions & scrolls (see Crafting): Minor / Greater Healing potions; Scroll of Warding (+2 defense, whole party) and Scroll of Wrath (+2 damage, whole party). Scrolls tick by wall-clock and persist through combat boundaries. Duration scales with artificer level (5 min base + 1 min per 2 levels). Active scroll buffs are shown near the compass with time remaining.
Weapons, armor, and shields are sold at the Wandering Tinkerer (50g per power/blocking, 150g shields). Sell any item at half price.
✨ Reagents (Tinkerer prices): Common 500g, Uncommon 2,000g, Rare 5,000g. They can also be found as monster drops.
🧣 Trinkets — cloak/neck/ring/belt accessories. Tier 1 available at the shop (150g × tier), higher tiers drop from monsters.
