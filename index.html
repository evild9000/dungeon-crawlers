Light & Darkness
The dungeon is genuinely dark — only what your party carries is lit. You start with 3 torches. When your light goes out, return to the Tinkerer or restock from monster drops.
🔦 Torch — 12-square radius, 10 minutes, warm flickering flame. Buy for 25g; 3% chance to drop from any slain monster.
🫙 Lantern + 🛢️ Lantern Oil — 24-square radius, 15 minutes per oil, steady golden light. Lantern 100g (reusable), Oil 30g (consumed).
✨ Light spell — mage only. 10 MP for an 14-square cool white light lasting 5 minutes.
Press T any time to pick a source. Only one light burns at a time; starting a new one replaces the old. Timers only tick during exploration (combat pauses them).
Press F outside combat to open Mage Familiar: eligible level 25+ mages can summon, name, and upgrade their permanent familiar.
Press N outside combat to open Shadow Simulacra: eligible level 30+ photomancers form permanent shadow allies from selectable powers, with templates for repeated builds.
Stat Bars
Red — Health (HP). When this reaches 0 the character falls.
Green — Stamina (ST). Powers melee and ranged attacks.
Blue — Mana (MP). Powers magic attacks.
Purple — Experience (XP). Fills as your character earns XP; resets each time they level up.
Attacking with insufficient stamina/mana still works, but deals half damage.
Levels & XP
Every character starts at Level 1 and can reach Level 100.
Defeating a monster grants 10 × its level XP, split evenly across all living party members (summons excluded).
Cumulative XP needed for level N is 100 × N² (400 for L2, 900 for L3, 1,600 for L4, …).
On level-up: +12 HP / +12 ST / +12 MP (scaled by class modifiers), and all pools refill to full.
Each class gains per-level scaling (see Classes below) — higher levels grant better damage, stun/crit/dodge chances, and special bonuses.
Combat Rows
Every character is either Front Row (⚔) or Back Row (🏹). Toggle from the personal inventory screen.
Monsters only target front-row characters with melee attacks. However, ranged and spell attacks can reach back-row characters directly.
When the entire front row is down, combat pauses and you must promote a back-row ally forward.
Only Rogues may melee from the back row (stealth). Everyone else is limited to ranged / magic / special abilities from the back.
Initiative & Turn Order
At the start of each combat, every participant — allies and enemies alike — rolls a d12 to determine turn order. Higher rolls act first; ties are broken randomly.
Class bonuses: Rogues, Monks, and Rangers roll at +1 initiative.
Initiative is re-rolled every round — all surviving combatants (allies and enemies) roll fresh d12s at the start of each round, so turn order can shift. Dead combatants are skipped. Monsters that join mid-combat as wanderers enter the initiative pool for the next round when they arrive.
Summons created mid-combat inherit their summoner's initiative and are inserted immediately after the caster's slot — they act this round, not the next.
Combat Mechanics
Defense = class + species + per-level bonus + armor + trinkets. Every point directly reduces incoming damage.
Melee attacks have a 20% base stun chance; warriors gain +3% per level.
Ranged attacks have a 20% base crit chance; rangers gain +3% per level. Ranged weapons roll +1 min/+1 max damage over other subtypes.
Magic attacks hit random targets — the number of hits equals the caster's level + 4 (mages get +2 extra hits). Mages also gain +1% stun per level and ignore enemy defense by level% on magic/AoE hits.
Warlock Eldritch Bolt replaces the normal random-target magic attack: it targets one enemy, bypasses armor/defense, and gains +level% damage.
Rogue Backstab: 3× stamina for 2× damage plus +10% per rogue level (+100% at L10). Rogues can also attempt to spot and disarm traps. At L25, successful disarms recover a Captured Trap for combat use, and rogues can evade magic/AoE attacks at level% chance for 2 stamina.
Equipped weapons add their power to matching attack types.
Equipped armor reduces incoming damage by its blocking value. See Armor Restrictions below.
Equipped shields have a base 25% chance to completely block a melee, ranged, or magic/AoE hit (legendary shield: 40%). Each Artificer shield enchant rank (+1 to +7) raises block chance by an additional +1% and adds +1 defense. Shields cannot be used with a ranged weapon equipped, and Mages/Monks/Necromancers/Artificers/Warlocks cannot use shields at all.
Warrior Defend Mode (L20) — A warrior in Defend Mode intercepts attacks targeting any party member (melee, ranged, or magic/AoE). The warrior rolls their augmented block chance (shield base + 1%/3 warrior levels); on a hit, the original target takes no damage and the warrior absorbs 40% of the post-defense damage instead. Toggling Defend Mode on/off costs the warrior's action that turn. While in Defend Mode, the warrior's own melee, ranged, and magic attack buttons are all disabled — only special/passive abilities remain available.
Warrior Stun Resistance — Warriors have a passive level × 2% chance to shrug off any stun (caps at 95% at level 47+). A shrugged stun is fully negated and the warrior acts normally that turn.
Encounter size: combat always pulls in enough adjacent monsters to match your party size (summons included).
Fallen allies are not auto-revived at the end of combat — use a Resurrection Potion to bring them back.
Status Effects
🟢 Poison — ticks damage at the start of each of your rounds for several turns. Spiders and some monsters apply it on hit. A bigger dose refreshes and supersedes a weaker one. Poison from traps persists out of combat and continues ticking every 10 seconds of exploration time. Poisoned characters show a green 🤢 face badge on their HUD portrait.
🐍 Venom (Poisoned — weapon rider / spider bite) — a stronger poison variant: deals DoT per round and reduces the target's melee/ranged damage output while it persists. Applied by the Poison weapon enchantment and spiders. A newer, stronger dose always replaces a weaker one.
✨ Stun — the target loses its next action. Some character abilities and some monster abilities can stun. Weapon riders (⚡ Lightning and ❄️ Ice) also stun the target for 1 round when they proc. Regular monsters have an increasing chance to shrug off stun. Bosses and Mega-Bosses are immune to stun. Undead, constructs, elementals, and incorporeal enemies are completely immune to stun.
⚡ Shocked (Lightning rider) — stunned for 1 round and attack power reduced for several rounds. Enemy deals less damage while shocked.
❄️ Chilled (Ice rider) — stunned for 1 round and defense reduced for several rounds. Enemy takes extra damage while chilled.
❄️ Ice Chill — applied by ice trolls and yetis on melee hits: reduces the target's damage output for 2 rounds. Does not stun.
🔥 Burning — a fire DoT applied by the Fire weapon enchantment, fire drake / red dragon breath, and the monk Avatar (fire element). Deals damage per round for 2 rounds. Immune (on enemies): fire-affinity creatures only — drakes, imps, fire elementals, fire giants, efreetis, and similar monsters with innate fire resistance (check their lore entry). Mummies take double burn damage.
🟢 Acid — an acid DoT applied by the Acid weapon enchantment and black dragon breath. Deals damage per round and reduces the target's defense while active.
🟥 Bleed — a bleed DoT from rogue Backstab (L20+) and wolf summon melee hits. Deals damage per round for floor(rogue level / 5) rounds. Immune: undead, constructs, elementals, incorporeal enemies.
🍃 Entangle (druid) — roots an enemy: its defense is reduced, so your party deals extra damage for the duration. L30 — Verdant Surge: each entangled enemy also suffers nature DoT each round equal to a random druid magic attack; 25% chance per round to lose its action (not bosses/mega-bosses). Incorporeal enemies are immune to the Verdant Surge DoT.
👁 Evil Eye (Warlock) — free-action hex usable once per round. It lowers one enemy's defense and melee/ranged/magic output by floor(warlock level / 3), minimum 1. It lasts at least 1 round and costs 1 MP per round to maintain.
🌀 Blur (Photomancer L6) — party-wide light distortion. Melee and ranged attacks against blurred allies have a 20% chance to miss outright.
👁 Invisibility (Photomancer L6/L20) — invisible allies cannot be selected by melee, ranged, or single-target magic attacks; AoE still hits. Normal Invisibility breaks when the target damages an enemy. Improved Invisibility does not break on attack.
🕯️ Wasting Curse (Warlock L20) — magic curse on one target. It deals 1% of current HP at first, rises by 1% each round to a 5% cap, and has a 5% chance per round to be shrugged off. Magic-immune targets are immune, and only one can affect a target at a time.
🧚 Fae Poison (Faerie Queen) — a magic DoT applied by the Faerie Queen's Wrath of Nature. Bypasses physical resistances. Shown as 🧚 badge on enemy card.
🧚 Hold (Treant / Faerie Queen) — Hold effects immobilize the target for 1–2 rounds (turn skipped). Only incorporeal enemies are immune to holds — undead, constructs, and elementals can all be held. Player-controlled swarms are immune to all holds, including death roll. Sources: Treant slam (33%/hit, 1 round), Faerie Queen Wrath of Nature (33%+/hit, 2 rounds). Shown as 🧚 Held badge on enemy card.
🟤 Mummy Rot — rot applied by mummy summons (permanent DoT) and monster mummies (3-round healing block). While active, the target's healing and regen are completely suppressed; on bosses/mega-bosses, reduced by 50% instead. Immune: undead, constructs, elementals, and incorporeal (no living flesh to decay). Necromancers in Lich Form are immune to monster-inflicted Mummy Rot.
🧟 Paralysis (Ghoul) — a distinct CC category with the same immunity profile as stun: undead, constructs, elementals, and incorporeal are all immune. Ghoul paralysis immobilizes the target for several rounds (turn skipped each round). Bosses and mega-bosses are immune. Unlike Hold, which only incorporeal can resist, Paralysis is blocked by the same creature types that are immune to stun.
🌊 Drowning (Water Elemental AoE) — the whole party is engulfed in a surging torrent. Applies a drowning DoT for 3 rounds and a separate armor-weakening debuff that reduces defense for 3 rounds.
🤨 Ghost Fear — applied by Ghost summons and Demi-Liches. Permanently reduces the target's attack and defense by 3 each for the rest of the combat. Shown as 😨 Feared badge.
✝️ Turned — applied to undead enemies by cleric Turn Undead. Reduces the target's attack and defense for several rounds. Shown as ✝️ Turned badge.
🎶 Disrupted (Bard Dissonant Chord) — one-use-per-fight AoE: deals magic damage to all enemies with a +2% damage bonus per bard level, applies a Disrupted debuff (−attack/−defense for 1 round), and gives each a 50% stun chance. The debuff scale rises by +1 every 5 bard levels.
🎵 Charmed (Bard Charm Monster) — a successfully charmed enemy switches sides for floor(bard level / 5) rounds, fighting hostile enemies on your behalf. Immune: undead, elementals, constructs, bosses, and mega-bosses.
✋ Quivering Palm (Monk L20) — a unique internal DoT planted by the monk's Quivering Palm strike. Starts at the base melee roll and multiplies by (2 + level÷100) each round (e.g. L30 = ×2.3, L100 = ×3.0), bypassing all armor and defense, for up to 10 multiplications. Multiple monks can stack separate Quivering Palm effects on the same target.
🧘 Pressure Points (Monk L30 passive) — each application of Quivering Palm by a L30+ monk also stacks a cumulative −1 defense debuff on the target. Expires when that monk's QP expires. Immune: undead, incorporeal, elementals, constructs.
🕸️ Webbed / Immobilized — the target cannot act for N rounds; its turn is skipped each round the effect persists. Applied by spider web attacks and naga constrict. Bosses and mega-bosses immediately tear free.
🪨 Petrified (Beholder / Medusa) — the target is turned to stone: immobilized for 3 rounds (turn skipped) and gains +200 defense while petrified, making them nearly impervious to damage during that time. Immune: incorporeal, construct, and elemental party members (golems, spectres, ghosts, and undead summons cannot be petrified).
🐌 Slow Ray (Beholder) — reduces a front-row target's melee attack and defense by dungeon level / 2 for 2 rounds.
🔮 Anti-Magic Ray (Beholder) — reduces a target's magic damage output by 10 for 2 rounds.
🐺 Wolf Totem Bleed (Ranger wolf totem) — applied on ranged and explosive arrow hits while the wolf totem is active. DoT per round for floor(ranger level / divisor) rounds. Immune: undead, constructs, elementals, incorporeal.
🧚 Pixie Totem Poison (Ranger pixie totem) — applied on ranged and explosive hits while the pixie totem is active. A standard poison DoT per round. Immune: undead (no living biology), and any enemy with explicit poison immunity (fire elementals, certain demons — check their lore entry).
🎯 Hunter's Mark (Ranger L30 free action) — costs 5 ST + 5 MP to place on a single enemy. The marked target receives +15% damage from all sources for as long as the mark holds. Upkeep: 3 MP + 3 ST per round; fades if resources run out. If the ranger personally kills the marked target, the ranger gains a free bonus turn — on that turn, the mark can be placed again for free, potentially chaining kills. A 🎯 badge appears on the ranger's HUD card when a mark is active.
🦎 Beastlord (Ranger L30 free toggle) — toggle on for 5 MP/round base. While active, at the start of each round the ranger rolls a (level/2 + 10)% chance to automatically summon a random beast (wolf, bear, eagle, or pixie). Auto-summoned beasts follow all normal summon rules including wolf packs and upgrades (Giant Bear, Golden Eagle, Pixie Princess). Each auto-summoned beast costs 1 additional MP/round upkeep. Beastlord turns off when mana is exhausted. A 🦎 badge appears on the ranger's HUD card when active.
🪤 Trap Wound (Rogue trapcraft L25) — springing a captured trap on the enemy group applies a wound DoT to all enemies for 3 rounds equal to 50% of the initial melee damage. A newer trap wound overwrites an older one. Immune: incorporeal enemies.
🗡️ Twin Fangs (Rogue L30 passive) — when an offhand melee weapon is equipped, every Backstab automatically triggers a second offhand follow-up strike. The offhand hit rolls fresh offhand weapon damage ×1.5 with the same level scaling as backstab — it does not use the ×2 backstab multiplier. Instakill chance is half the normal backstab instakill chance (e.g. if backstab is at 8%, the offhand is 4%). Each strike has its own bleed DoT (L20+) and weapon rider procs. No chain: the offhand strike does not trigger further Twin Fangs.
🌑 Shadow Step (Rogue L30, 1×/combat, 50 ST) — the rogue vanishes for 2 combat rounds. While active: completely untargetable (all enemy attacks, AoE, and status debuffs pass through harmlessly); Backstab damage is multiplied by ×2 on top of all other bonuses including Twin Fangs. A 🌑 badge on the rogue's HUD card shows remaining rounds.
⚙️ Drone Binding (Artificer combat drone) — the drone fires a binding shot that reduces the target's attack and defense for several rounds. Immune: incorporeal enemies (bindings pass through spectral forms) and bosses/mega-bosses (too powerful to be ensnared).
🧘 Avatar DoT (Monk L25 Avatar) — while Avatar is active the monk's landed melee attacks (except Quivering Palm) apply a chosen elemental DoT based on the selected element (Fire / Air / Earth / Water). Damage and duration depend on monk level.
⚔️ Spiritual Weapon (Cleric L30) — an undamageable back-row weapon of pure force summoned by a level 30+ cleric (10 MP to summon, 3 MP/round upkeep). Fires floor(cleric level/8) force attacks per round against random enemies. Damage is based on melee weapon roll + magic class bonus. Multiple weapons can be active simultaneously. Immune to all status effects, DoTs, holds, and stuns. Cannot be targeted or damaged by enemies. Disappears when the cleric dies or combat ends.
✨ Banishment (Cleric L30, 50 MP) — a powerful divine attack against elementals and demons only. Cleric level% chance to instantly destroy the target. Bosses and mega-bosses cannot be instantly destroyed but still take the full ×20 holy force damage bypassing all defense. On survival: deals magic roll ×20 holy force damage ignoring all armor and resistances. Unlimited uses per combat; limited only by mana cost.
Armor & Shield Restrictions
Cloth only: Mage, Necromancer, Monk
Cloth / Leather: Rogue, Druid, Artificer (no chain or plate)
Cloth / Leather / Chain: Ranger, Bard (no plate)
All armor: Warrior, Cleric, Paladin
No shields: Mage, Monk, Necromancer, Artificer. A shield is also forced off whenever you equip a ranged weapon or enable dual-wielding.
Dungeon Levels & Portals
The dungeon has up to 100 floors. Deeper floors spawn tougher monsters that deal more damage and drop more gold.
🔴 Red portal — descends to the next floor. Appears on every level.
🟢 Green portal — ascends to the previous floor. Appears on levels 2 and deeper.
Step through a portal to travel to the next (or previous) dungeon floor.
Exploration
Walk into enemies — or let them walk into you — to begin combat. The enemy you touch becomes the primary monster. Initial combat size is capped at one enemy per real party member + one extra per 10 dungeon levels. The fight starts with the primary monster plus up to 5 fresh monsters from its primary tag group. If room remains, nearby hostile map monsters are pulled in immediately by square rings out to 10 grid squares; each pulled monster becomes a secondary encounter type and brings up to 3 fresh monsters from its own tag group. After rings 3, 6, 9, and 10, any remaining capacity is filled with packs of 3 primary-tag monsters plus 1 from each secondary tag until the cap is reached or no spawn cells remain. Monsters not pulled into the initial group can still wander in later: at the start of each new combat round, every eligible hostile enemy nearby rolls a distance-weighted chance (50% base, +5% per round elapsed, minus 5% per grid square of distance). Bosses and mega-bosses never wander into an ongoing fight.
The Wandering Tinkerer roams the dungeon. Bumping into them opens a shop (no combat).
Rest at any time in safety to recover (R). Resting costs 1 food per living party member (golems don't eat). Keep food stocked!
⚠️ Traps: invisible pressure plates lurk on some floor tiles. Types are randomised per trap — pit, swinging blade, dart volley, arrow trap, falling rocks, poison spray, acid jet, fire blast, lightning rune, choking gas, ceiling crusher, scything pendulum, snare net, spring spikes. If you have a Rogue in the party, they may spot the trap before you step on it and you can attempt to disarm it — success scales with rogue level. Without a rogue, you trigger the trap and the whole party takes damage. A modal dialog announces every sprung trap, every successful disarm (revealing any hidden treasure), and every failed disarm. Poison-kind traps (darts, poison spray, choking gas) also apply a poison DoT.
Regeneration
All characters slowly regenerate while exploring: +1 HP/min, +3 ST/min, +2 MP/min by default.
Class and species add to these rates. Regen also continues while menus are open, but not during combat.
Food & Hunger
Every non-summoned party member needs to eat (golems and other summons are excluded). Food is checked every 15 minutes of real exploration time.
When the check fires, each hungry character automatically consumes 1 food — drawing first from their personal inventory, then from the group inventory if their personal supply is empty.
If no food is left, hunger escalates through three stages:
🟡 Hungry — minor stat penalty (−1 to all attacks and defense).
🟠 Starving — significant penalty and stamina drain.
🔴 Dying — characters lose HP each minute until they eat or fall.
The hunger badge appears on each character's portrait in the HUD. Buy food at the Tinkerer (50g) or find it as monster loot. Resting also requires food.
Classes
Each class has unique stats, per-level bonuses, and level-gated abilities.
Monster Tags & Special Powers
Some monsters carry tags that unlock class interactions. Mouse over an enemy card in combat to see its tag line.
🧟 Undead — vulnerable to paladin Smite (bonus damage & instakill chance), AoE Smite, and Divine Judgment (L30, % current HP). Targeted by cleric Turn Undead (2× magic damage + debuff). Necromancer life-drain rules also apply. Immune to poison, stun, paralysis, bleed, and mummy rot. Can be held by treant/faerie queen.
😈 Demon — vulnerable to paladin Smite, AoE Smite, and Divine Judgment (bonus damage & instakill chance). Also targeted by cleric Banishment (L30): cleric-level% instant destruction, or ×20 holy force damage on survival. No inherent status immunities by tag.
🐉 Dragon — targeted by paladin Dragonslayer (L25): Smite and AoE Smite also hit dragons; active Dragonslayer with shield reduces dragon magic/AoE damage to the whole party by (paladin level + 10)%, capped at 90%. Also targeted by Divine Judgment when Dragonslayer is active. No inherent status immunities by tag alone (individual dragons carry specific elemental immunities).
🐺 Beast — wild creatures. Ranger and druid summons count as beasts. No inherent status immunities by tag.
🧑 Humanoid — intelligent mortal creatures (orcs, goblins, cultists, giants, dark elves, etc.). Can be Charmed by bard Charm Monster (L20). No inherent status immunities by tag.
🧱 Construct — artificial beings (gargoyles, golems, fire elementals). Immune to Bard Charm Monster (no mind to enchant). Immune to poison, stun, paralysis, bleed, and mummy rot. Can be held by treant/faerie queen. If also incorporeal, additionally immune to web, hold, entangle, and petrification.
🔥 Elemental — beings of pure elemental energy (fire, air, water, earth elementals). Immune to Bard Charm Monster. Vulnerable to cleric Banishment (L30). Immune to poison, stun, paralysis, bleed, and mummy rot. Individual elementals carry specific elemental immunities (e.g. fire elementals immune to fire). If also incorporeal, immune to web, hold, entangle, and petrification.
👻 Incorporeal — spectral creatures (spectres, ghosts, certain elementals) that pass through physical bindings. Cannot be Entangled, immune to web, hold, paralysis, stun, petrification, and drone bindings. Also immune to bleed and mummy rot. Undead incorporeal creatures (spectre, ghost) are additionally immune to poison. Can still be damaged normally.
🕷️ Vermin — small scurrying creatures (spiders, rats, bats, centipedes, cave fishers, wasps). No special class interactions or tag-based immunities. Often carry poison or web abilities as individual traits.
👾 Monster — catch-all for unclassified creatures (mimics, spore fungi, shriekers, harpies, nagas, gelatinous cubes, etc.). No special class interactions or tag-based immunities.
🧠 Aberration — alien or eldritch entities that defy natural classification (mind flayers, tentacle horrors, beholders, star spawn). No special class interactions or tag-based immunities by tag alone, but individual aberrations often carry powerful unique abilities.
Boss Monsters
A 👑 Boss may occasionally spawn — a crowned variant of a normal monster with dramatically increased power.
Bosses have 5× HP, +dungeonLevel attack and defense. Their name appears with a crown (👑) in the combat log and enemy card. Bosses take 2 actions per round.
Defeating a boss grants 5× gold, 5 loot rolls (weapon, armor, trinket, reagents — all rolled 5 times), and a 3× trinket drop chance per roll. Bosses also guarantee an extra 1–2 rare reagents.
💀 Mega Boss — extremely rare encounter (deeper floors). Mega Bosses are dramatically stronger than regular bosses: massive HP, very high attack and defense, and take 3 actions per round. Defeating a Mega Boss grants 10× gold, 5 guaranteed loot rolls, and 2 of every qualifying reagent tier.
Boss & Mega Boss immunity: Bosses and Mega Bosses are completely immune to stun, hold, paralysis, and all player-inflicted CC effects (including weapon riders, Faerie Queen hold, Bard Dissonant Chord, etc.). They are also immune to instant-kill effects — Backstab and Smite/AoE Smite purge still deal heavy bonus damage instead. Plan accordingly: focus on raw damage and party sustain.
👑 Boss Aura (minion buff): While a Boss is alive, all other monsters in the same fight deal +25% damage. While a Mega Boss is alive, allies deal +50% damage instead. These effects stack if both are present. Affected enemies display a 👑 +25% dmg or 💀 +50% dmg badge on their card. Killing the Boss or Mega Boss removes the aura immediately — prioritize them to protect your party.
Crafting (K)
Press K while exploring to open the Crafting menu (requires an Artificer in the party). Four tabs: Enchant, Trinkets, Potions & Scrolls, Golems.
Reagents come in seven tiers — ✨ Common / 🔵 Uncommon / 🟣 Rare / 🔶 Epic / 🌟 Legendary / 💜 Mythic / 💫 Divine — each rolling independently per monster slain. Common drops everywhere; Uncommon at DL 4+; Rare at DL 7+. Base 5% chance per tier, +1%/level up to 10%. Epic (DL 15+), Legendary (DL 20+), Mythic (DL 25+), and Divine (DL 30+) have a flat 1% drop rate with no level bonus — but bosses guarantee 1 of each qualifying tier, and mega-bosses guarantee 2. Also sold at the Tinkerer (Common / Uncommon / Rare only). Higher tiers unlock stronger golem tiers and enchant levels +4 through +7.
Weapon Enchants — upgrade any equipped weapon to +1 / +2 / +3 (each step flat +damage). After reaching +1 you may attune a single elemental rider (25% proc on melee/ranged hits):
  • 🔥 Fire — 1.5× extra burn DoT for 1 additional round.
  • 🧪 Acid — DoT plus enemy defense debuff.
  • 🟢 Poison — DoT plus enemy damage debuff.
  • ⚡ Lightning — 1-round stun + attack (damage) debuff.
  • ❄️ Ice — 1-round stun + defense debuff.
Armor Enchants — +1 / +2 / +3 to an equipped armor piece (flat bonus defense). Unequipping clears the enchant.
Shield Enchants — +1 to +7 ranks on an equipped shield (same cost as armor). Each rank adds +1% block chance (stacks with the shield's base chance) and +1 defense. Unequipping clears the enchant.
Trinket Crafting — Artificers L20+ can upgrade equipped trinkets up to +7; dual-aspect trinkets cost twice as much because both bonuses improve. At Artificer L25, effective level 4+ trinkets can also receive a separate vitality augment with its own level and cost track. Augment levels 4/5/6/7 add +5%/+10%/+15%/+20% to the wearer's max HP, stamina, and mana.
Potions & Scrolls:
  • 🧪 Minor Healing Potion — restores a portion of max HP to one character.
  • 🧪 Greater Healing Potion — restores more HP to one character.
  • 🛡️ Scroll of Warding — read aloud to grant the entire party bonus defense. Bonus = +2 base, +1 per 5 artificer levels (AL5→+3, AL10→+4, AL15→+5 …). Craft cost: 2 common reagents base, +1 common per bonus point above +2; +1 uncommon per +5 total bonus; +1 rare per +10 total bonus. Duration: 5 min + 1 min per 2 artificer levels. An active buff icon appears near the compass with time remaining.
  • 🔥 Scroll of Wrath — read aloud to grant the entire party bonus damage to all attack types. Same bonus scaling and cost formula as Scroll of Warding. Duration: 5 min + 1 min per 2 artificer levels. An active buff icon appears near the compass with time remaining.
Golems (unlocked by Artificer level; all stats scale with AL at time of forging — melee 2–12 + floor(AL×1.5), defense AL×2):

Attack Resolution Order

How an Enemy Attack Is Resolved
Every enemy attack — melee, ranged, or magic/AoE — passes through a fixed sequence of checks before damage lands. Each check either ends the attack early (no damage) or falls through to the next. Damage reductions that modify the raw number do not end the chain. The phases below are evaluated in strict order.
Phase 1 — Raw Damage Setup
The monster rolls its base damage, then three global multipliers are applied before any defense is checked.
Boss Aura All types — multiplier Each other living boss in the enemy group adds +25% to raw damage; each mega-boss adds +50%. These stack.
Paladin Dragonslayer Aura Dragon magic/AoE — reduction If a paladin has Dragonslayer active and has a shield equipped, dragon magic and AoE damage is reduced by (paladin level + 10)% for the whole party before anything else, capped at 90%.
Per-Level Monster Scaling Melee / Ranged — multiplier Melee attacks gain +2% per monster level; ranged attacks gain +1.5% per monster level. Magic damage is not scaled here.
Phase 2 — Pre-Defense Reductions
These checks reduce or deflect damage before the target's armor or defenses are counted. Eagle Totem and Vampire Gaseous Form end the attack entirely; the others only reduce the raw damage value.
Shadow Step All types — full miss A rogue in Shadow Step is untargetable. The attack passes through harmlessly — no damage, no riders, no follow-ups.
Eagle Totem Deflect Ranged only — full miss + reflect Any ranger with Eagle Totem rolls level × rate% (cap 95%). On success, the shot is reflected back at the attacker for a fraction of the raw damage. Attack ends; target is unharmed.
Ranger Pixie Totem Resist Magic / AoE — reduction If the target is a ranger with Pixie Totem active, incoming magic and AoE damage is reduced by a flat percentage.
Druid Pixie Wild Shape Resist Magic / AoE — reduction A druid in Pixie wild shape takes 50% less magic and AoE damage.
Armor AoE Ward AoE only — reduction A non-summoned target with an armor enchant at +4 or higher with the AoE Ward quality reduces AoE damage: +4 = 10%, +5 = 20%, +6 = 30%, +7 = 40%.
Vampire Gaseous Form All types — full immunity A vampire summon already in gaseous form is completely immune to all incoming damage.
Paladin Aura of Righteousness (L30) All types — reduction Reduces raw damage by the paladin's aura percentage. Applied here so that intercepting warriors also benefit from the reduction.
Phase 3 — Personal Defense Checks
Checked in strict order. A successful check ends the attack immediately (no damage, no riders). A failed check falls through to the next — all eligible checks are always evaluated regardless of whether prior ones failed. Each check is an independent roll.
Druid Eagle Wild Shape Evasion Ranged only A druid in Eagle wild shape rolls level × rate% (cap 95%) to evade a ranged attack.
Rogue AoE / Magic Evasion (L25) Magic / AoE only Costs 2 ST. Roll level% (cap 100%). On success, the rogue sidesteps the spell entirely.
Mirror Image (Mage L20) All types If the mage has mirror images active, one image absorbs the hit and shatters. Works against any attack type.
Monk Active Dodge Melee only Costs ST + MP. Roll dodge%. On success: no damage; if Ki unlocked, generates 1 Ki charge. If this roll fails but the monk still has resources, the Monk Passive Reduction still applies later in damage calculation.
Shield Block Melee, Ranged, Magic/AoE Rolls the target's shield block chance against all attack types. Base 25% (legendary shield: 40%); each Artificer enchant rank adds +1% (max +7%, for up to 32% / 47%). Does not apply to phase strikes. Mages, Monks, Necromancers, and Artificers cannot equip shields.
Death Knight Spectral Shield Melee, Ranged, Magic/AoE Flat 25% block chance for death knight summons against all attack types.
Golem Shield Attachment All types A golem summon with a shield attachment rolls its block chance against any attack type.
Squire Shield All types Squire summons have a flat 25% block chance against any attack type.
Pixie / Pixie Princess Dodge All types Pixie summons roll 25% + summoner level% (cap 90%) to dodge any attack.
Phase 4 — Interceptors
Checked after all personal defenses. This ordering ensures that a target's own dodge, block, or evasion is always tried first — the interceptor only steps in when the target cannot stop the blow themselves. Phase strikes from certain enemies bypass both interceptors entirely.
Vermin/Acid Swarm Intercept All types — checked first When a Vermin Keeper has Swarm Protect active and a living Vermin or Acid Swarm is present, the swarm rolls 50% + (keeper level / 300) to step in front of the keeper. The swarm takes the full raw damage. Checked before the Shambling Mound because swarms are considered more expendable.
Shambling Mound Intercept All types — checked second A druid's shambling mound summon rolls 50% + (druid level / 300) to step in front of its summoner. The mound takes the hit (raw damage minus mound armor and defense). Checked before the warrior because summons are disposable.
Warrior Defend Mode Intercept All types — checked third Each warrior in Defend Mode (not the target) rolls their augmented shield block chance. On success, the warrior steps in front: the target takes no damage; the warrior takes 40% of post-defense raw damage (min 1). Multiple warriors are tried highest-HP first. After a successful intercept, the warrior may retaliate (L25+).
Phase 5 — Damage Calculation
If the attack reaches this phase it will deal damage. Reductions are applied in the order listed.
Phase Strike Bypass Certain enemies Incorporeal or phasing attackers set armor and defense to 0. The attack log reads "phases through defences." Rage, monk passive reduction, and defending-mode halving still apply.
Defending Mode Halving All types If the target used the Defend action this round, damage is halved before armor and defense are subtracted.
Armor Blocking All types The target's equipped armor value is subtracted from incoming damage (minimum 1 after all reductions). Bypassed by phase strikes.
Defense All types The target's total defense (class + species + levels + trinkets + summon bonus) is subtracted from incoming damage. Bypassed by phase strikes.
Barbarian Rage Halving All types A raging barbarian takes half damage after armor/defense. Cannot be bypassed by phase strikes.
Monk Passive Reduction All types Even when the active dodge roll failed (or was ineligible), a monk with both ST and MP above 0 reduces damage by their current dodge%. Magic/AoE attacks use 2/3 of the dodge % for this reduction. Cannot be bypassed by phase strikes.
Lich Form Magic Resist Magic / AoE only A necromancer in Lich Form resists magic/AoE damage: base 50%, +1% per 4 levels above L20, capped at 90%.
Faerie Queen Magic Resist Magic / AoE only Faerie Queen summons take 50% less damage from magic and AoE hits.
Adamantine / Divine Soul Golem Magic, AoE, Ranged These golem types take half damage from magic, AoE, and ranged attacks.
Bard Thunderous Drums (L30) Sonic / Psychic only A bard with Thunderous Drums active reduces sonic and psychic attack damage by up to bard level%.
Phase 6 — Damage Application & Post-Hit Effects
Barbarian Temp HP (L20) All types Battle Fury temp HP absorbs incoming damage first. If fully absorbed, the real health pool is untouched. Remaining damage bleeds through to real HP.
HP Damage Applied All types Final damage is subtracted from target HP. Minimum 1 after all reductions. Special on-death effects (Lich Phial, druid wild shape exit, vampire gaseous form) trigger here.
Paladin Fire Aura Counter-Strike Melee only If the target is a paladin with Fire Aura active, the attacker takes fire damage equal to the paladin's rolled melee damage, ignoring the attacker's defense.
Armor Spikes Reflect Melee only Spiked armor enchant (+4→25%, +5→50%, +6→75%, +7→100%) reflects the post-mitigation damage value back at the attacker.
Clay Golem Reflect All types Clay golem summons have a percentage chance each hit to reflect a fraction of post-mitigation damage back at the attacker.
Secondary Riders Melee only (unless noted) If the target survives: Poison (typeDef poisonChance), Stun (typeDef stunChance), Web / Paralysis (typeDef webChance / paralyzingBite), and other monster-specific melee riders (constrict, life drain, etc.) are each rolled independently. Undead and incorporeal summons are immune to several of these.
Monster Follow-Up Attacks (Dungeon Level 25+)
Starting at dungeon level 25, every primary melee or ranged hit that successfully connects (passes all defense checks and deals damage) rolls up to five additional follow-up attacks. Each tier is rolled independently — a failed roll does not stop the remaining tiers from being attempted.
Follow-up attacks go through the full Phase 3–6 resolution (target can dodge, block, etc.). They inherit the same attack type (melee follow-ups target the front row; ranged follow-ups target any alive party member) and include boss damage bonuses. Secondary riders (stun, poison, web, etc.) also apply on follow-up hits.
2nd Attack DL 25+ Chance = dungeon level ÷ 100 (e.g. DL 50 = 50%, DL 100 = guaranteed)
3rd Attack DL 25+ Chance = dungeon level ÷ 200 (DL 100 = 50%)
4th Attack DL 25+ Chance = dungeon level ÷ 400 (DL 100 = 25%)
5th Attack DL 50+ Chance = dungeon level ÷ 800 (DL 100 = 12.5%)
6th Attack DL 80+ Chance = dungeon level ÷ 1600 (DL 100 = 6.25%)
Boss damage bonuses per hit: Regular boss +DL×3 (melee/ranged) or +DL×2 (magic); Mega-boss +DL×4 or +DL×3; Super-boss +DL×4.5 or +DL×3.5.
Example at DL 100: a monster with 3 base attacks per turn has a guaranteed 2nd follow-up per hit, 50% chance of a 3rd, 25% for a 4th, 12.5% for a 5th, and 6.25% for a 6th. Expected total hits: 3 × ~2.94 ≈ 9 per turn. Maximum possible: 18 hits.
